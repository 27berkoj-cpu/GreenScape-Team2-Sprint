Aeration & Seeding

Improve soil health, boost turf growth, and revitalize your lawn with expert aeration services.

What is Soil Aeration?

Soil aeration is the process of improving the exchange of gases between the atmosphere and the soil. It helps the earth breathe by creating more air and space in the soil profile.

This process is essential for strong root development, better water infiltration, and increased nutrient uptake by turfgrass and landscape plants.

Why is Soil Aeration Important?

Aeration relieves soil compaction, allowing oxygen, water, and nutrients to reach the root zone more effectively.

  • Relieves Soil Compaction: Loosens dense soil so roots can grow deeply.
  • Enhances Gas Exchange: Improves oxygen availability for healthy root respiration.
  • Improves Water Infiltration: Helps rain and irrigation penetrate evenly.
  • Boosts Nutrient Uptake: Makes fertilizers and minerals more accessible to grass.
  • Supports Thatch Breakdown: Encourages microbial activity that decomposes dead organic material.

What is Hydroseeding?

Hydroseeding is a fast and efficient way to establish new turf by spraying a slurry of seed, mulch, fertilizer, and water onto prepared soil.

This method is ideal for large areas, slopes, and sites where quick and uniform grass coverage is desired.

Hydroseeding Slurry Ingredients

The hydroseeding mix is designed to protect seed, retain moisture, and promote rapid germination.

  • Grass Seed: A custom blend selected for your climate and lawn conditions.
  • Mulch: Helps the seed stay in place and retain moisture.
  • Fertilizer: Supplies nutrients for early growth.
  • Water: Makes the mixture easy to apply and keeps everything hydrated.
  • Tackifier: Binds the slurry to the soil surface so it stays put.

What is Overseeding?

Overseeding places new grass seed into an existing lawn to fill in bare spots, thicken turf, and improve overall density.

This service revitalizes lawns that have become thin, patchy, or stressed by weather and traffic.

Why Overseeding Matters

  • Increases Turf Density: Gives your lawn a thicker, healthier appearance.
  • Reduces Weeds: New grass helps crowd out unwanted plants.
  • Improves Resilience: Introduces stronger grass varieties for a durable lawn.
  • Enhances Color: Fresh grass seed boosts vibrancy and uniformity.
  • Prevents Erosion: Denser turf helps hold soil in place.
